# 🚀 Project Features & Roadmap

This document outlines the current capabilities of the Antigravity Task Manager and the planned roadmap for future enhancements.

---

## ✅ Current Features

### 🔐 Authentication & Access
- **Multi-Provider Auth**: Support for traditional email/password and one-click Google OAuth.
- **Role-Based Security**: Strict Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring users only see data they are authorized to access.
- **Organization Hierarchy**: Manage multiple teams and projects with isolated data environments.

### 📋 Task Management
- **Kanban Board**: Visual task tracking through custom status columns.
- **Priority Matrix**: Categorize work by urgency (Low, Medium, High).
- **Deadlines**: Integrated due-date tracking with automated overdue indicators.

### 📎 Collaborative Tools
- **File Attachments**: Securely upload and share documents up to **25MB**.
- **Real-Time Sync**: Instant UI updates for member lists and task attachments using WebSockets.
- **Threaded Comments**: Real-time discussion boards for every task to keep communication contextual.

## 🗺️ Future Roadmap (Extra Features)

1.  **👤 Task Assignment**
    *   *Description*: Ability to assign one or more team members to a specific task.
    *   *Why*: Improves accountability and helps in workload balancing across the team.

2.  **🔔 Real-Time Notifications**
    *   *Description*: In-app and email alerts for mentions, assignments, and updates.
    *   *Why*: Ensures team members stay informed without constantly checking the app.

3.  **📜 Activity Log**
    *   *Description*: A permanent audit trail of all changes made to a task or project.
    *   *Why*: Critical for transparency and understanding the history of a task's progress.

4.  **📑 Task Templates**
    *   *Description*: Pre-configured sets of tasks for common workflows (e.g., "New Employee Onboarding").
    *   *Why*: Saves time and ensures consistency across recurring processes.

5.  **♻️ Recurring Tasks**
    *   *Description*: Automated task generation for daily, weekly, or monthly duties.
    *   *Why*: Eliminates the manual work of recreating the same tasks repeatedly.

6.  **✅ Subtasks & Checklists**
    *   *Description*: Hierarchical breakdown of large tasks into smaller, trackable items.
    *   *Why*: Helps manage complex requirements and provides a clearer sense of progress.

7.  **⏱️ Time Tracking**
    *   *Description*: Native timers to log hours spent directly on individual tasks.
    *   *Why*: Essential for agencies and teams tracking billable hours or productivity.

8.  **📊 Analytics Dashboard**
    *   *Description*: High-level charts showing team velocity, completion rates, and project health.
    *   *Why*: Data-driven decision making for managers and team leads.

9.  **🔍 Global Search**
    *   *Description*: Universal search bar to find tasks, projects, or comments across the whole app.
    *   *Why*: Speed up navigation in large organizations with hundreds of tasks.

10. **🔌 Slack/Discord Integration**
    *   *Description*: Sync task activities to external team communication channels.
    *   *Why*: Meets teams where they already work, reducing "tool fatigue."

11. **🌗 Dark Mode Improvements**
    *   *Description*: Enhanced theme engine with custom accent colors and better contrast controls.
    *   *Why*: Improves accessibility and allows users to personalize their workspace.

12. **📅 Calendar View**
    *   *Description*: A drag-and-drop calendar interface for scheduling and deadlines.
    *   *Why*: Provides a better temporal understanding of project timelines.

13. **📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)**
    *   *Description*: Optimized mobile experience with offline support and home-screen installation.
    *   *Why*: Enables "on-the-go" task management without a native app store download.

14. **🛠️ Custom Fields**
    *   *Description*: Add user-defined fields (e.g., "Estimate", "Client ID") to tasks.
    *   *Why*: Flexibility to adapt the tool to specific industry needs (Legal, Dev, Marketing).

15. **📥 Data Export**
    *   *Description*: Export reports and task lists to CSV, JSON, or professional PDF formats.
    *   *Why*: Useful for stakeholder meetings and external data processing.
